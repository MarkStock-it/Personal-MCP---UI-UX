# Ferro: a one-man bicycle frame workshop in Cebu that builds steel frames to order

## Audience
Serious commuters and touring riders, 28 to 50, who have outgrown mass-market bikes and distrust glossy brand sites. They read specs and will wait months for the right frame.

## Tone and voice
Dry, confident, a little rude. Talks like a mechanic, not a marketer.

## Content
Founder: Dante Alcala, 14 years welding. Frames are TIG-welded 4130 chromoly, built to the rider's measurements. 6 to 9 week wait. Prices from 62,000 PHP. 41 frames built in 2025. Slogan idea: "Built once. Fixed forever."

## Goals
Get a visitor to book a 20-minute fitting call. Success is 10 booked calls a month.
